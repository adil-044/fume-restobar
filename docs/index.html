The place

A neighbourhood hangout that grew up into a destination.

"Reinventing the neighbourhood hangout — one flame at a time."

In Brantford's Holmedale, Fume built its kitchen around the two things you can't fake: fire and smoke. Smoked pork belly gets honey dijon and pickled fennel; sirloin gets cowboy butter; feta gets whipped and blasted with spicy bomba honey.

The room is small by design — exposed brick, flicker from the pass, music that warms up as the evening does. Greek fusion at the table, a wine list and bar worth the evening, and gluten-free handled with actual excellence.
