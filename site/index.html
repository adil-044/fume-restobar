Fire & smoke
Smoked. Fired.

Smoked pork belly with honey dijon. Steak frites under cowboy butter. Whipped feta blasted with spicy bomba honey.

The place
A neighbourhood
hangout that grew up.

In Brantford's Holmedale the kitchen is built on the two things you can't fake: fire and smoke. The room is small by design — exposed brick, the pass flickering, music that warms as the evening does. Greek fusion at the table, a wine list worth the night, gluten-free handled with actual excellence.
