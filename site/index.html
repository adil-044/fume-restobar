01 The place

A neighbourhood hangout that grew up into a destination.

“Reinventing the neighbourhood hangout — one flame at a time.”

In Brantford's Holmedale, the kitchen is built on the two things you can't fake: fire and smoke. Smoked pork belly gets honey dijon and pickled fennel; sirloin gets cowboy butter; feta gets whipped and blasted with spicy bomba honey.

The room is small by design — exposed brick, the pass flickering, music that warms as the evening does. Greek fusion at the table, a wine list worth the night, and gluten-free handled with actual excellence.
